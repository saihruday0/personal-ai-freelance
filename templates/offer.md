# Initial Freelance Offer

## Who I help
Online businesses and creators who lose time handling repetitive enquiries, collecting leads, managing content workflows, or maintaining their websites.

## What I offer
A combination of a professional, responsive website or landing page and one clearly scoped business automation.

## Examples
- A landing page with a structured enquiry form.
- A website enquiry workflow that organizes incoming leads.
- A creator website with a content enquiry or booking workflow.
- Automated notifications or reports using the client's existing tools.

## Delivery process
1. Understand the client's problem and requirements.
2. Research the existing workflow and propose a solution.
3. Prepare the design and technical specification for approval.
4. Agree on scope, price, timeline, and payment terms.
5. Build, test, and demonstrate the solution.
6. Obtain approval and deliver the agreed work.

## Constraints
- No promises of guaranteed revenue or results.
- No work outside the agreed scope without approval.
- No paid services or subscriptions without explicit authorization.
- No publishing or sending client communications without approval.
