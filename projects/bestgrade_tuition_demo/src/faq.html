COMMON QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know.

Explore the questions below. If you need further information, please contact the centre.

What age groups does Bestgrade support?

The website describes tuition for children from age 4 onwards, covering learning stages from Reception through advanced-level programmes. Please contact the centre to confirm availability for a particular age group.

What subjects and programmes are available?

The listed programmes include Primary tuition, 11 Plus preparation, Secondary tuition, GCSE, IGCSE, A-Level and adult courses. Availability and subject coverage should be confirmed directly with the centre.

Are classes taught in groups or individually?

The website describes both group classes and one-to-one tuition. It also mentions small classes of approximately 5–6 students. Contact the centre to discuss the most suitable arrangement.

How is a student's progress monitored?

The website describes progress tests every six weeks. These assessments help review a student's progress and identify areas that may need additional attention.

Do you offer support for 11 Plus examinations?

Yes. 11 Plus preparation is among the programmes listed on the website, including preparation classes, mock exams and past-paper classes.

When do tuition sessions take place?

The website describes tuition during weekdays after school, weekends and holidays. Please contact the centre for current timetables and available sessions.

How can I find out about tuition fees?

Fees depend on the programme and learning arrangement. Visit the programmes and fees page for the prices displayed in the existing programme listings, and contact the centre to confirm current fees.

How can I get started?

The existing website describes an initial assessment process. Contact the centre to ask about assessment availability, programme suitability and enrolment.
